OUR STORY

It started with coffee on an ordinary afternoon in Manila. What was intended to be a brief meeting turned into hours of shared laughter, quiet understanding, and the effortless realization that we had found home in one another.

Through years of shared dreams, travels, and everyday moments, our bond has only grown deeper. Now, surrounded by those we love most, we are excited to begin our greatest chapter yet as husband and wife.
